SC1 Rules

sc1simon – ONE ROUND MMA BATTLE 1v1

Official competition rules for the SC1 one-round format, designed for controlled full-contact competition with fighter safety as the priority.

1. Format

  • The match consists of one round with no time limit.
  • The bout may be decided by knockout, technical knockout, choke submission, or referee stoppage.
  • The referee may stop or conclude the match at any time in the interest of fighter safety.
  • The match is conducted as controlled full contact competition under these rules.
  • Participation is voluntary and based on informed consent.

2. Equipment

  • Mandatory MMA gloves, provided by SC1.
  • Mandatory mouthguard, provided by fighter.
  • Mandatory groin protection, provided by fighter.
  • Optional hand wraps, provided by fighter.

3. Permitted Techniques

  • Punches, elbows, kicks, knees and headbutts.
  • Wrestling, clinch work, takedowns, and positional control.
  • Choke submissions.
  • Pulling clothes.
  • Grabbing the fence.

Prohibited Techniques

  • Joint locks or limb submissions.
  • Strikes to the back of the head, spine, groin, or throat.
  • Knees or kicks to the head of a grounded opponent (grounded as in any of the following parts touch the ground: knee, elbow, buttocks).
  • Eye gouging, biting or pulling hair.

The referee has final authority over rule enforcement.

4. Safety and Consent

  • Fighters compete only while conscious and able to defend themselves.
  • If a fighter loses consciousness or cannot intelligently defend, the referee will immediately stop the match.
  • No further contact is permitted after a stoppage.

5. Compensation

  • Each fighter receives participation compensation of 500 SEK / 50 EURO upon entering the match and competing in good faith.
  • An additional discretionary performance bonus of 500–2500 SEK / 50–250 EURO may be awarded based on overall performance and competitiveness.
  • Total potential compensation per fighter ranges between 500–3000 SEK / 50–300 EURO.
  • A fighter may voluntarily stop the match at any time, including while still capable of continuing. Doing so results in a loss and forfeits participation compensation. Safety and freedom of choice take priority.

6. Matching and Conduct

  • Fighters are matched as closely as possible in weight and skill level.
  • Standard maximum weight difference is 5 kg unless mutually agreed.
  • Unsportsmanlike conduct may result in disqualification.
  • All participants acknowledge the inherent risks of full contact competition and must sign a waiver prior to competing.
